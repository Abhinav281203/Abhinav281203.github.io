Ant-colony-system

The Ant Colony System (ACS) for the Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) is a technique inspired by how real ants find the shortest paths to food. The process involves a form of reinforcement learning (RL), where ants learn from their previous actions, updating their behavior to improve future decisions. The above is based on Marco Dorigo's paper: Ant Colony System: A Cooperative Learning Approach to the Traveling Salesman Problem. (Updated on Aug 27, 2024)
